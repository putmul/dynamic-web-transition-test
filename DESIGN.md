## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an optimistic, human-centered technological clarity. Tailored for conscious digital citizens, educators, students, and urban tech-forward communities exploring everyday green innovations, the interface bridges digital precision with organic stewardship.

### Aesthetic Direction: Modern Eco-Precision
The visual language merges crisp architectural minimalism with subtle tactile vitality. We reject artificial, muddy earth tones in favor of vivid plant-inspired emeralds, solar accents, and clinical neutral slates. Interfaces prioritize luminous breathing room, crisp tabular data, precise micro-indicators, and structured interactive containers. 

### Emotional Response
- **Informed & Empowered:** Environmental data is delivered with scientific authority, stripping away gloom in favor of actionable intelligence.
- **Fresh & Vital:** The interface breathes through light slate-white surfaces, vibrant chlorophyll-charged highlights, and unburdened layouts.
- **Trustworthy & Technical:** Crisp typography and deliberate spacing anchor carbon footprints, SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure), and SDG 13 (Climate Action) metrics within credible digital architecture.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from living ecosystems calibrated against laboratory precision:

- **Primary (`#059669` - Emerald Vibrant):** Drives forward motion, confirmed status, key calls to action, and interactive state triggers. Represents dynamic, active sustainability.
- **Secondary (`#064E3B` - Deep Forest Slate):** Anchors high-priority headlines, key metric outputs, dark container cards, and brand grounding. Conveys architectural permanence.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7` - Clean Hydro/Solar Cyan):** Used for clean energy indicators, technological breakthroughs, interactive calculator inputs, and SDG metrics (specifically SDG 9 clean technology & SDG 13 climate mitigation tags).
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Neutral):** Provides balanced optical contrast across technical borders, structural dividers, secondary body copy, and muted background surfaces (`#F8FAFC` base background, `#F1F5F9` input/card wells).
- **Functional Semantics:** Soft amber-gold (`#D97706`) is reserved strictly for real-time solar yield highlights and quiz achievement stars; destructive red (`#E11D48`) denotes high-emission warnings.

## Typography

Typography pairs technical ingenuity with friendly humanistic cadence:

- **Space Grotesk (Headlines & Labels):** Anchors the eco-technological atmosphere. Its geometric structure, monospaced proportions, and distinct technological letterforms provide punchy, modern authority to emission counters, headings, and data badges.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body):** Delivers clean readability and soft friendliness to long-form sustainability tips, interactive explanations, and quiz prompt scenarios.
- **Technical Readouts & Calculators:** Real-time numerical calculations always deploy `Space Grotesk` tabular figures to avoid layout jitter during live calculation updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system runs on an 8-point spatial rhythm within a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (4-column layout, `margin-sm`, `gutter-sm`)
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (8-column layout, `gutter`, `margin`)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12-column layout, max-width `1280px` centered, `margin: 2rem`)

- **Interactive Calculator Layout:** Two-column split on desktop (5 columns for dynamic input sliders; 7 columns for dynamic environmental impact visualizers). Stacks vertically into input-first, output-second on mobile.
- **Quiz Flow:** Single-column centered container constrained to a maximum width of `680px` to maintain focused cognitive alignment without visual fragmentation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layer nesting** complemented by **organic ambient tinted shadows** and low-opacity slate micro-borders:

- **Surface Levels:**
  - **Base Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` (Cool slate tint).
  - **Surface 1 (Cards, Modules):** `#FFFFFF` bordered with `1px solid rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.12)`.
  - **Surface 2 (Calculators, Modals, Quiz active cards):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ambient tinted shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08)`.
  - **Surface Inset (Interactive slider tracks, input wells):** `#F1F5F9` with subtle inner drop shadow `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.

- **Elevation States:**
  - **Hover:** Active cards elevate by `translateY(-2px)` accompanied by expanding green ambient diffusion: `0 16px 28px -6px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.14)`.
  - **SDG & Category Badges:** Flat, tint-backed surfaces (10% opacity primary fill) with sharp 1px borders; no elevation shadows to maintain technical tag utility.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape language:
- Standard buttons, form controls, and badge elements adopt `0.5rem` (8px).
- Content cards, quiz containers, and calculator modules adopt `1rem` (16px) corner radii.
- Modals, banner highlights, and large summary dashboards utilize `1.5rem` (24px).
- Pill geometry (`rounded-full`) is reserved strictly for numeric metric chips, filter tags, and SDG badge labels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid emerald background (`#059669`), white text, bold `Space Grotesk` font. Active transitions provide an emerald glow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** 1.5px border in `#064E3B`, slate-dark text, transparent fill; fills with 5% `#064E3B` on hover.
- **Calculator Trigger / Reset:** Ghost button styling with subtle `#64748B` label and primary icon accent.

### Sustainable Energy Badges (SDG 9 & SDG 13)
- Compact pill-shaped containers with an ultra-light colored surface (`rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.08)` for SDG 13; `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08)` for SDG 9).
- Left-aligned indicator icon (e.g., leaf, solar cell, clean gear) paired with uppercase `label-badge` text tracking.
- Bordered with an identical 20% opacity matching hue line.

### Interactive Calculator Panels
- Structured container backed by Surface 1 with an inset well for sliders and form toggles.
- Sliders: Clean 6px height track (`#E2E8F0`), vibrant filled progress segment in `#059669`, with a crisp 20px circular white thumb accented by a 2px emerald rim.
- Readout Area: Prominent high-contrast dark green panel (`#064E3B`) featuring vivid emerald numbers (`code-numeral`), sub-labels in `#94A3B8`, and instant carbon reduction metric comparisons (e.g., "equiv. to 12 trees planted").

### Quiz Cards
- Self-contained cards with clear question headers and a 4-choice response list.
- Option Rows: Inactive state features `#F8FAFC` background with a neutral border. Active/Hover shifts to `#ECFDF5` with an emerald border. Correct answer triggers a solid `#059669` outline with a subtle checkmark badge; incorrect response triggers `#FEE2E2` with a rose badge.
- Sticky progression bar at the top of the quiz deck filled with dynamic emerald gradient.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- Height `44px`, base background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Focus State: Smooth outline shift to `#059669` backed by a `0 0 0 3px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15)` focus ring.
- Affixes: Suffix badges (e.g., "kWh", "kg CO2e", "km/hari") rendered in uppercase `label-md` Space Grotesk.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes: 20px squared elements with 4px border-radius. Inactive border `#94A3B8`; active state fills `#059669` with white SVG checkmark.
- Radio Buttons: Custom concentric ring indicator in emerald green on select, ensuring clear feedback for single-option quiz and audit flows.
